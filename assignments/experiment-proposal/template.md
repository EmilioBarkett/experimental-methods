## Format A: TESS-style (survey experiments)

Follow the TESS (Time-sharing Experiments for the Social Sciences) proposal
structure: short description, treatment(s), outcome measure(s), and any
covariates, sized to a survey-experiment budget/format. Check TESS's current
submission guidelines directly for the exact template.

## Format B: Pre-analysis plan / OSF pre-registration (non-survey experiments)

Follow an OSF-style pre-registration structure: research questions,
hypotheses, design, sampling plan, variables, and analysis plan, specified
before data collection. Check OSF's current pre-registration templates
directly for the exact fields.
